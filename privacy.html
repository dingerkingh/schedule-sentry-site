Schedule Sentry Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026 · Developer: Daren Hunter · Bundle ID com.darenhunter.Schedule-Sentry

  • No accounts, no sign-up. Schedule Sentry uses only the iCloud account already on your device. No passwords, payments, or in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, ads, tracking, or third-party SDKs. The app contains no third-party code and talks only to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit).
  • Your calendar data stays on your device or in your own iCloud. The developer runs no servers and cannot read your private iCloud data.

Data we access on your device, and why

  • Calendars (full access). Read to find upcoming events on the calendars you turn on, so alarms can be created before them. Event titles and times name the alarms.
  • Reminders (full access). Only for the "Reminder" alert type: the app creates reminders in the Reminders app (titled after the event, with an ss-key: marker in the notes) and removes the ones it created when they are no longer needed.
  • Alarms (AlarmKit, iOS). For the "Loud alarm" type. Alarms carry only the event title and time; a Live Activity shows the same on the Lock Screen.
  • Notifications. Optional "Get ready" notices before an event (title plus "Starts at …"). Permission is requested only when you set up preparation alerts.
  • Background refresh and silent pushes. The app re-checks calendars in the background and receives silent iCloud sync pushes so alarms stay current.
  • macOS. Alerts appear as a floating card with a sound. "Launch at Login" is optional and under your control.

Data stored on your device

  • Which calendars you watch and how each alerts (also mirrored to iCloud, below).
  • A ledger of loud alarms scheduled on this iPhone or iPad (event title, times, alarm ID). Never synced.
  • A local cache of household data and iCloud sync state.
  • A random device ID generated by the app (not your device name, serial number, or advertising ID).

Data synced through iCloud (CloudKit)

iCloud is the only place data leaves your device. It lives in the CloudKit container iCloud.com.darenhunter.Schedule-Sentry under your Apple Account, protected by Apple; the developer has no access to it.

In your private iCloud database (only you and your devices)

  • Watched-calendar settings: calendar title, account name (e.g. "iCloud"), on/off, alert type, lead time, snooze, reminder list, overrides.
  • Your alert defaults, per platform.
  • Per-event overrides and cancelled alarms: event title, event date, an event identifier, and the setting.
  • A "calendar changed" nudge per device: the random device ID, platform ("iOS"/"macOS"), and timestamps.

In a household you create or join (shared with household members through iCloud sharing)

  • Household: name, chosen shared calendar title, dates, and the owner's iCloud user record ID (an opaque CloudKit identifier, not a name or email).
  • Members: the display name you typed, your iCloud user record ID, join date, your alert timing preferences, and which shared calendar your devices have seen.
  • Assignments: the assigned event's title, start/end time, all-day flag, calendar name, an event identifier, who assigned it, who it is assigned to, dates, and status. Removed assignments are marked "cancelled" but retained.
  • Receipts: per device, whether the alarm was armed (or why not, e.g. "alarm permission denied"), the random device ID, timestamps, and any error code.

Who can see it: every household member sees all of the above, including each member's display name, alert preferences, assigned event titles, and whether their devices armed the alarm. Only people the owner invites can join; the share is never public. Apple's iCloud sharing delivers invitations and handles participant identity.

Push notifications

The app registers for CloudKit's silent pushes to sync in the background. Every visible alarm or notification is generated on your device from your own calendar data. The developer cannot send you notifications.

Third parties

None. No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking. Apple (iCloud/CloudKit) stores your data under Apple's privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

  • Revoke calendar, reminder, alarm, or notification access any time in Settings > Privacy & Security (iOS) or System Settings > Privacy & Security (macOS). The app stops reading those sources.
  • Reminders the app created live in your Reminders app; delete any that remain there.
  • Deleting the app removes all local data. Data already in your iCloud persists until you delete it: Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Schedule Sentry > Delete Data (macOS: System Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage).
  • Household data is stored in the owner's iCloud. Removing an assignment keeps a cancelled record. The owner can delete the household from the Family tab (Delete Household), which deletes every household record and every member's access from iCloud. A member can leave from the same place (Leave Household), which deletes their member record and receipts, cancels events assigned to them, and removes their access; the owner keeps the rest of the household. Deleting the owner's app data in iCloud (above) also removes the household for everyone.
  • Signing out of iCloud or switching accounts wipes the household cache on that device.
  • The developer holds no copies of your data, so there is nothing to retain or delete on a server.

Children

Schedule Sentry is a household tool, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; families that include children use the app through the parents' iCloud accounts and Apple Family Sharing.

Your rights

Because the developer does not collect or hold your personal data, most requests can be fulfilled on your device using the steps above. If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you may still contact us to ask what data we hold (none on our servers), request deletion, or raise a concern. We do not sell or share personal information.
